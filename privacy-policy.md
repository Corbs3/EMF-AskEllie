# Ask Ellie privacy policy

**Last updated:** 22 October 2026

Ask Ellie is a Chrome extension that helps you find and understand circular economy terms on web pages.

## Summary

**Ask Ellie does not collect, send, share or sell any personal data.** Everything stays in your own Chrome browser.

## Page content

- Ask Ellie only reads a page when you ask it to, by clicking the toolbar icon, pressing Scan or using the right-click menu.
- The page is checked inside your browser. Page text is never saved or sent anywhere.
- Short snippets around each term are shown in the side panel so you can see the context. They are kept only until the page changes or Chrome closes.

## What is stored

- **Theme choice** (light, dark or auto): stored locally in your browser.
- **Custom terms you add**: stored with Chrome sync, so they appear in your own Chrome browsers if Chrome sync is turned on.
- **Scan results for each tab**: stored for the current session only, and removed when the page changes, the tab closes or Chrome closes.

## What is not collected

- No browsing history
- No personal information
- No analytics or tracking
- No network requests: the extension does not connect to any server

## Third parties

No data is shared with any third party.

## Deleting your data

- Delete custom terms from the custom terms page (cog button in the side panel).
- Removing the extension deletes all of its stored data.

## Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
